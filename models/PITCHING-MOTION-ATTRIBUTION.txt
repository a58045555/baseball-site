Local changes: parsed BVH using forward kinematics, discarded the initial
reference pose, trimmed the captured action to source seconds 2.0 through 4.35,
sampled at 60 Hz, normalized the standing reference height to 1.8 metres,
rotated the captured stride toward +Z, translated the original floor to zero,
and rounded joint coordinates to 0.00001 metre for compact web delivery.
No captured joint rotations or limb lengths were edited.

This is an actor's recorded demonstration of pitching, not an individual
player's technique, a prescribed coaching form, or measured professional data.

3D pitcher (dist/models/pitcher-pro.glb, runtime dist/pro-pitcher.js)
- Character: generated by the site owner with Meshy AI (text-to-3D, A-pose, pinstripe uniform without any
  logo or text) on 2026-10-22 and supplied to this project as a GLB. This site's own "B" badge and the number 17
  are added at runtime. The original 20 MB file is not part of the repository (tools/meshy rebuilds the rigged
  GLB from it with landmarks-apose.json).
- Local work (tools/meshy/): mesh decimation (gltf-transform), skeleton and skin weights built from measured
  landmarks (autorig.mjs), logo removal (paint.py), GLB assembly (assemble.mjs); pitcher's glove from
  dist/scene-models.js.
- Rig: Mixamo-style bone names (65 bones). Pitching motion retargeted in world space with two-joint IK, ground
  contact for planted feet, forearm twist sharing and a four-seam ball grip.

Three.js r180, GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180 (MIT, see vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt).
Pitching animation: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 124, motion 01. See PITCHING-MOTION-ATTRIBUTION.txt.

Earlier characters kept for reference only: tools/pro (Mixamo "Michelle" repaint, Adobe Mixamo terms),
dist/chibi-pitcher.js (code-built cartoon), tools/build-pitcher.mjs + tools/legacy/skinned-pitcher.js
(MakeHuman/MPFB2 CC0 athlete, license in MAKEHUMAN-MPFB2-CC0-LICENSE.txt).

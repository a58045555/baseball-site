3D pitcher (pitcher-athlete.glb)
- Body mesh, macro shape targets, "game_engine" rig and skin weights: MakeHuman base mesh via MPFB2,
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2 (src/mpfb/data). Assets are CC0 1.0 Universal; the license is
  included as MAKEHUMAN-MPFB2-CC0-LICENSE.txt.
- Built locally by tools/build-pitcher.mjs: body shape (young adult male, lean athletic), one Catmull-Clark
  subdivision, and the cap, bill, jersey, pants, shoes and hair as separate modelled meshes generated from
  the body's own topology. Brows, lips, piping, numbers, belt, socks and undershirt are drawn by shaders.
- Eyes, cap button, pitcher's glove (dist/scene-models.js) and ball are modelled in code.

Three.js GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180/examples/jsm (MIT, see vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt).

Pitching animation: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 124, motion 01. See PITCHING-MOTION-ATTRIBUTION.txt.
Retargeting: capture scaled to the athlete's leg length, two-joint IK, ground contact for planted feet, forearm twist sharing, ball grip.

3D pitcher (dist/chibi-pitcher.js)
- Built entirely in code with Three.js primitives: a chibi-style cartoon athlete (big round head,
  compact body, round joints, mitten hands) with cel shading and ink outlines. No downloaded model.
- Pitcher's glove, catcher's mitt, ball and field props: dist/scene-models.js, also built in code.

Three.js r180, GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180 (MIT, see vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt).

Pitching animation: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 124, motion 01. See PITCHING-MOTION-ATTRIBUTION.txt.
Retargeting: capture scaled to the cartoon's leg length, two-joint IK, ground contact for planted feet, forearm twist sharing.

Earlier realistic model (no longer shipped): tools/build-pitcher.mjs builds a MakeHuman/MPFB2 (CC0) athlete;
tools/legacy/skinned-pitcher.js is its runtime. MAKEHUMAN-MPFB2-CC0-LICENSE.txt is kept for that tool.

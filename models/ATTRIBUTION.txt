3D pitcher (dist/models/pitcher-pro.glb, runtime dist/pro-pitcher.js)
- Character: "Michelle" by Mixamo (Adobe), taken from the three.js examples
  (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Michelle.glb). Mixamo characters may be
  used royalty-free in your own projects under the Adobe Mixamo terms; the character itself is not redistributed
  as a standalone asset.
- Local work (tools/pro/): headphones, sunglasses and hair puffs removed; diffuse map repainted into a baseball
  uniform (jersey, collar, belt, pants, socks, cleats) while keeping the original shading; cap, bill, badge and
  jersey numbers modelled in code; pitcher's glove from dist/scene-models.js.
- Rig: the Mixamo skeleton (65 bones). Pitching motion retargeted in world space with two-joint IK, ground
  contact for planted feet, forearm twist sharing and a four-seam ball grip.

Three.js r180, GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180 (MIT, see vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt).
Pitching animation: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 124, motion 01. See PITCHING-MOTION-ATTRIBUTION.txt.

Earlier characters kept for reference only: dist/chibi-pitcher.js (code-built cartoon), tools/build-pitcher.mjs +
tools/legacy/skinned-pitcher.js (MakeHuman/MPFB2 CC0 athlete, license in MAKEHUMAN-MPFB2-CC0-LICENSE.txt).
